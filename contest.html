FAQ

Who can enter?
The contest is dedicated to software developers ready to innovate with the Wavebinder library.
Eligibility Requirements:
  • Age: You must be at least 18 years old.
  • Location: You must be a resident or have a registered office in Italy.
  • Profile: Open to individuals, freelancers, and companies.
How to participate?
You can enter as a solo developer or as a team. Teams must designate a lead representative. Please note that each participant or team can submit only one project.
How long does the contest run?
The competition is divided into two main phases:
  • Registration Phase: You can sign up and submit your project (an Empty Public GitHub Repository Link) from March 1, 2026, to April 30, 2026.
  • Development: You can start working on your project on May 4, 2026. The project must be completed by October 5, 2026, and the final submission must include a demo video uploaded to the GitHub repository.
How is the winner selected?
After the submission deadline, a technical Jury composed of at least three experts will evaluate all projects based on a 100-point scale:
  • Integration & Use of Wavebinder (Max 50 pts): Complexity of data models, effective use of features, and stability.
  • Technical Quality & Architecture (Max 20 pts): Code cleanliness and adherence to best practices.
  • UX & UI (Max 20 pts): Ease of use and design quality.
  • Originality & Utility (Max 10 pts): Creativity of the concept.
When will the results be announced?
The Jury will meet to assign the awards by December 18, 2026. Winners will be notified via email (at the address provided during registration) and must accept the prize within 5 days.
